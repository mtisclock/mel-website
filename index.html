People not buildings.

Trust is a design decision.

Rethinking how retrofit programmes are designed, delivered and experienced by the people they’re supposed to serve.

Lochend, north east Edinburgh

People before property

When good intentions go wrong.

I live in Lochend, north east Edinburgh, an area of high deprivation. Since 2023 a government-funded retrofit programme has been rolled out across our many homes, including mine.

Despite good intentions,the programme design did not follow the reality of what a low income community needs. For many of us this has resulted in debt, poor quality works, damage, and in some cases eviction. Others have been left with no improvements at all.

About Mel

The person behind the practice

People
are the point.

I’m Mel. I work at the intersection of service design, public programmes and people’s lived experience.

I’m interested in what changes when we design public services around the realities of people’s lives — rather than expecting people to fit the programme.

I’ve worked in social housing, energy efficiency and construction. For the last 3 years I’ve been campaigning on retrofit with Living Rent and talking to industry and councils about what I’ve seen so that this does not happen to others. My Churchill Fellowship report, on what happens when residents are trusted to take part in retrofit: https://www.churchillfellowship.org/ideas-experts/ideas-library/people-not-buildings-trust-empowerment-and-resident-participation-in-urban-retrofit-and-adaptation/
