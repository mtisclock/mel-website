01 / Case study

Lochend

I live in Lochend, in north east Edinburgh. Since 2023 a government-funded retrofit programme has been rolled out across our neighbourhood, an area of high deprivation. It started with good intentions but the programme design did not follow the reality of what a low income community needs. For many of us this has resulted in debt, poor quality works, damage, and in some cases eviction. Others have been left with no improvements at all.

Walk round Lochend today and you can see buildings that were never upgraded, because the people who had a choice said no.

I’m one of the residents directly affected by this programme and that is what drives my work.

02 / Now

What I’m working on

Retrofit means asking millions of households to say yes, and people only say yes to what they trust. Without it, programmes can’t scale.

No trust, no scale.

Lochend is what that looks like on the ground. Most retrofit programmes start with the surveys and the budget, and trust comes in at the end, when it’s usually too late. I want to do it the other way round: trust first, before anything is designed. I call it pre-design trust.

I am working on two specific questions: How do we build trust and the right practices into retrofit programmes? And if we did start early, how do we stop it turning into a box to tick?

03 / About

Background

I’ve worked in social housing, energy efficiency and construction. For the last 3 years I’ve been campaigning on retrofit with Living Rent and talking to industry and councils about what I’ve seen so that this does not happen to other communities. I trained in service design, which is how I approach most things.
