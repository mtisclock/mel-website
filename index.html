01 / Place & people

Lochend

What does it take for people to shape the changes happening around them? Lochend is a place to explore what resident participation looks like in practice, and what it takes to build relationships before decisions are made.

This work is about making space for lived experience, taking people seriously, and recognising that the process matters just as much as the physical outcome.

Lochend — project film

02 / In practice

What I'm working on

I work across service design, programme design and research to help people navigate complex change. My focus is on the relationships, conditions and decisions that shape how change is experienced by the people living with it.

In urban retrofit and adaptation, that means looking beyond buildings and technical solutions. It means asking who has influence, whose knowledge counts, and how residents can exercise meaningful agency in decisions that affect their lives.

03 / Perspective

Background

My practice brings together service design, research and programme design, with a particular interest in participation, trust and the relationship between institutions and the people they serve. I work across disciplines to make complex challenges more human, more legible and more open to change.

04 / The idea

People Not Buildings

Retrofitting buildings is also about changing people's homes, routines and futures. People Not Buildings makes the case for trust, empowerment and resident participation to be treated as essential parts of urban retrofit and adaptation — not optional extras once the technical decisions have been made.

05 / Sharing ideas

Talks and training

I speak, facilitate and deliver training on resident participation, service design, trust-building and people-centred approaches to urban change. I am interested in creating practical spaces for teams to question assumptions and work differently with the people affected by their decisions.
