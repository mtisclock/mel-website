Mel Esquerre

I work across service design, programme design and research to help people shape the places and services that affect their lives.

My focus is on resident participation, agency and trust — especially when decisions are being made about our homes and neighbourhoods.

01 / Case study

Lochend

What does it take for residents to have a meaningful role in decisions about the places they live?

My work explores how participation can begin with listening, relationships and trust — rather than asking people to respond once the important decisions have already been made.

02 / Now

What I’m working on

I’m interested in how services, programmes and research can create the conditions for people to participate with confidence and influence decisions that affect them.

My work connects lived experience with the systems, organisations and decisions that shape everyday life.

03 / About

Background

My practice brings together service design, research, programme design and participation. I work with complexity by making space for different perspectives, building shared understanding and helping turn insight into action.

04 / Point of view

People Not Buildings

**You cannot retrofit trust.**

Retrofitting and climate adaptation are not only technical challenges. They are also questions of power, relationships and whose knowledge counts.

People Not Buildings is about putting residents, trust and empowerment at the heart of decisions about the built environment.
